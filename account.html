One Account, Every System

  • The Game: the same username and password log you in through the launcher.
  • Your Characters: gear, jobs, crafts, nation rank and points at a glance.
  • Your Items: inventory, Mog Safe, Locker, Satchel, Sack, Case, all eight Wardrobes and the delivery box.
  • The Auction House: your listings, what sold, what you bought, and price history for any item.
