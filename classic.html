Classic at heart, kinder to your time

All of the nostalgia, none of the old frustrations.

The level 75 game you remember, with the friction sanded off. Everything that made Vana'diel special stays; the waiting and the busywork don't.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

What stays classic

Rooted in the era you love

Timeless starts from the level 75 game and builds on it rather than replacing it.

  • The level 75 cap, and the climb to get there
  • Vana'diel itself: the original areas, Rise of the Zilart, Chains of Promathia and Treasures of Aht Urhgan, with their timeless stories and nostalgia
  • Each job's classic role: the new mechanics deepen it, they don't replace it
  • The sights and sounds you remember, from the music of Ronfaure to the bustle of Jeuno
  • Old-school Dynamis, with out-of-era drops removed

Kinder to your time

ChangeWhat it means for you
Fast home point travelQuick warps between home points.
Timed notorious monstersLottery NMs spawn on a timer instead of from placeholders. See them all.
Fair alliance EXPAlliance members get the full party share of experience.
Chains of Promathia EXP bonusWith Signet, +5% to +10% experience in CoP areas from level 50.
Access key items for everyoneOne player can open Garlaige's gates, Quicksand Caves' doors and the Horutoto portal.
Thief positioningSneak Attack and Trick Attack work from any angle.
The Rewind HourglassBuy back the experience a knockout cost you, or return to where you fell.
Generous stack sizesHundreds of items that never stacked in the original era, from ores to beastmen's spoils, now stack up to 12, so your bags stay tidy and inventory management is far kinder.
Item adjustmentsThe Dusk set no longer slows you down, and large fish stack to 12.

Room for every job

All 8 Mog Wardrobes

Every adventurer has all eight Mog Wardrobes from the first day: 80 slots each, 640 in all. No unlock quests, no storage slips to juggle.

  • A full set for every job. Keep each job's gear in its own wardrobe and change jobs without a trip to the Mog House.
  • Equip straight from your wardrobes, anywhere in Vana'diel, just like from your inventory.
  • Your bags stay free for loot, crystals and crafting materials.

Connected from day one

The Timeless linkshell

Every adventurer shares one server-wide linkshell from their first login, with its own hourglass pearl. Help is always one message away.

The Timeless XI Launcher

One click to play, curated Ashita add-ons, graphics settings, and an automatic fix that keeps your macros and settings saved, with backups.

Your characters at a glance

Log in to the launcher to see each character: a 3D portrait, gear with in-game item cards, job levels, crafts and points.
