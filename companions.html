Companions

Never Alone

Your party is one call away. And they remember you.

Step outside the gates and call them: a tank, a healer, a support and two blades, playing their jobs like veterans. Keep the ones you trust, shape who they become, and send them where you can't go.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026

They fight like players

Not a script that swings and cures

They wait for the tank. They hold their weapon skill for your skillchain. When you fall, someone is already casting Raise.

Call a full party with !companion. Every companion plays at your level with their own gear, face and name, and reads the fight the way a seasoned player would.

  • Skillchains on purpose, and Black Mages waiting to burst them
  • Healers who pick the right Cure and never forget a Raise
  • Tank or damage, any support job, orders in party chat
  • Wyverns, jug pets, automatons and the right avatar

The bond

Fight beside the same faces long enough

Something changes.

Keep the companions you like and the same person answers every call. Every battle together deepens the bond, and every rank makes them stronger. A Sworn companion will spend their two-hour to pull you out of trouble.

  • Champion Slayers after ten Champions together
  • Errands: logging, mining and digging while you're busy

Companion Paths

Who will they become?

“We caught a Quadav scout on the road. Do we let him go?”

As the bond grows, your companions bring you choices. Mercy or iron, daring or care. Every answer earns a lasting perk, and by Sworn each one has a personality all their own. Then send them on a venture, and decide at the fork: the safe road, or the risky one?

!bond · !venture

Strangers on the road

Not everyone in the Dunes is a player

They're resting by the campfire. One of them waves.

Named adventurers camp across the levelling zones and linger in the towns. Chat, browse their wares, or ask one to join you. They fight with the same name and face, as a companion of their own job.
