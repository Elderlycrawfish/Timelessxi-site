Companions

Never wait for a party again.

AI companions fill out your party and play their jobs like seasoned adventurers. Call a full party in seconds, keep the ones you like, and grow stronger together.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Companions

A full party, whenever you need one

Step outside town and call them. Your companions are smart, capable adventurers who play their jobs like real players, at your level, each with their own gear and look.

Fill the party

Type !companion for a tank, a healer, a support and two damage dealers. Or call one job at a time with !war, !whm, !blm and the rest.

Each job has a 30-minute call cooldown

Point and click

Prefer windows to commands? /cm opens the companion manager to call and set up your party, and /cv shows every companion's gear, stats and bond.

On-screen windows from the launcher

Pets included

Dragoons bring a wyvern, Beastmasters the best jug pet for their level, Puppetmasters a named automaton. Summoners pick the avatar the monster is weakest to.

Pets come along automatically

They play like real players

Not a script that swings and cures

Companions read the fight. They wait for the tank to build hate, hold weapon skills to close a skillchain, burst on it with magic, and shed hate the way their job would.

  • Skillchains on purpose: they hold a weapon skill a few seconds to close the window, and Black Mages magic burst it.
  • Healers that think: the right Cure tier, Curaga for groups, status removal, and a Raise for anyone who falls.
  • Support that matters: Bards keep two songs up and Lullaby adds, Corsairs roll and Double-Up, Red Mages enfeeble.
  • Treasure Hunter: Thief companions stack Treasure Hunter on everything they hit.
  • Self-care: they rest, eat food by role and use potions and ethers, with no free regeneration.
  • They follow you: through zone lines, into battlefields, and back after a logout.

Keep your favorites

!companion keep saves a companion you like: up to 8, or 10 with extra slots from the Everglass Shop. From then on the same person answers every call for that job.

Bond through battle

Every worthy kill together adds bond, and Champions add far more. After 10 Champions together, a kept companion becomes a Champion Slayer, and Champions take 10% more damage from everyone.

Send them on errands

While you're busy, send kept companions logging, mining, harvesting, excavating, digging or helping the guild expedition for an hour. They come back with finds and more bond.

Your party, your rules

Presets

Save up to five line-ups and call one with !companion load, like "xp party".

Tank or damage, your call

Warriors, Paladins and Dark Knights can tank or deal damage, using their new specialties. !companion pld tank or dps

Pick their support jobs

Choose the support job each companion job uses, like Dark Knight with Samurai or Warrior with Ninja, and they put its abilities and spells to work in their role. Build the party that fits how you like to play. !companion sub drk sam

Party-chat orders

Type "hold", "go", "save mp", "nuke" or "go all out" in party chat, or ask for a spell: "cure me", "haste please".

Tactics and scouting

Set rules per job: interrupt, dispel, sleep, range, MP. !companion scout sizes up your target.

Fight report

!companion report shows damage, share, weapon skills, skillchains, bursts and healing for the last fight or the session.

Raise and retry

A fallen companion can be raised for 3 minutes. If nobody does, it goes home and its job cooldown is cleared.

Adventurers in the world

Living World

About 90 named adventurers camp in the levelling zones and stand around Jeuno, Selbina and Mhaura. They rest, wave, chat when you're close, and shout for parties in town.

  • Chat with them, or browse their wares: potions, food, crystals and ammo for their level.
  • Invite one to your party in the field. They join as a companion of their job, with the same name and face.
  • Their level has to be within 10 of yours, and you can bring one along every 20 minutes.

Better together

Full alliances

Companions can join an alliance. Three players with companions can field all 18 slots.

Fair alliance EXP

Alliance members get the full party share of experience instead of a reduced split.

Battlefields

Your companions rejoin you inside BCNMs and mission fights, as the same people.
