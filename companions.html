Companions

Never Alone

Your party is one call away. And they remember you.

Step outside the gates and call them: a tank, a healer, a support and two blades, playing their jobs like veterans. Keep the ones you trust, shape who they become, and send them where you can't go.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026

They fight like players

Not a script that swings and cures

They wait for the tank. They hold their weapon skill for your skillchain. When you fall, someone is already casting Raise.

Call a full party with !companion, or one job at a time. Every companion plays at your level with their own gear, face and name, and reads the fight the way a seasoned player would.

  • Skillchains on purpose, and Black Mages waiting to burst them.
  • Healers that think: the right Cure, Curaga for the group, a Raise for the fallen.
  • Your call: tank or damage, which support job, and orders in party chat like "hold" or "go all out".
  • Pets included: wyverns, jug pets, automatons and the right avatar for the fight.

The bond

Fight beside the same faces long enough

Something changes.

Keep the companions you like and the same person answers every call for that job. Every battle together deepens the bond, from Acquaintance to Sworn, and every rank makes them stronger.

  • Sworn companions use their two-hour on their own to pull you out of trouble.
  • Champion Slayers: ten Champions together, and your companion makes them all hit softer.
  • Errands: send them logging, mining or digging while you're busy, and they come home with finds.

Companion Paths and Ventures

Who will they become?

“We caught a Quadav scout on the road. Do we let him go?”

Every answer matters

As the bond grows, your companions bring you choices. Mercy or iron, daring or care. Each answer earns a lasting perk, and by Sworn, each one has a personality all their own.

!bond

Send them on a venture

Two or three companions set out on a mission while you play: a caravan to guard, a nest to clear, a Beastmen's Council to spy on. Halfway, word comes back. Safe road, or the risky one?

!venture

No two alike

Companions get different questions, so your Sworn Paladin and your friend's will never be quite the same.

Strangers on the road

Not everyone in the Dunes is a player

They're resting by the campfire. One waves.

Named adventurers camp across the levelling zones and linger in Jeuno, Selbina and Mhaura. Chat, browse their wares, or ask one to join you. They fight as a companion of their own job, with the same name and face.

Bring an army

Full alliances

Three players with companions can field all eighteen slots, and alliances earn full party experience.

Into every battle

Companions follow you through zone lines, into battlefields and back after a logout.
