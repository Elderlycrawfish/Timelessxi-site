Crafting & Trade

Every skill pays its way.

Crafting, gathering, digging and fishing level faster and pay off more, and everything you make or bring in feeds a guild economy with its own currency, Everglass.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Crafting, reforged

Every synth counts

Rested synths

Every hour away from crafting banks 2 rested synths, up to 50, each with better skill-up odds.

Flash of Insight

Now and then a synth sparks a moment of inspiration: a big jump in skill, or a much better chance of a high-quality result.

Lessons and mastery

A broken synth teaches you something; making the same recipe 25, 50 and 100 times raises its high-quality rate.

Craft orders and the craftbook

Each day brings three crafting orders matched to your skill, paying gil and rested synths. Type /craft to browse recipes, see what you can make with what you carry, and check auction prices.

The Artisans Guild

Lower Jeuno

AI crafters

Four crafters turn what players sell to vendors into goods on the auction house, and keep ammo, ninja tools and food in stock.

Commissions and requests

Bring ingredients and a fee for a craft that never fails. Every 3 hours each crafter posts requests that pay above NPC prices.

Gathering expeditions

A guild crew is always out in a beastman land for 2 hours. Donate gear, food or gil, or hunt beastmen there, and earn Everglass.

!guild · !expedition

Everglass

The server's own currency

Earn Everglass by supplying the guild's expeditions, filling guild requests and craft orders, finishing bounties and closing Time Rifts. Then spend it on what moves you forward.

  • The Everglass Shop (!shop): Champion lures, gatherer's focus, rested synths, the guilds' crafting gear, chocobo treats and extra companion slots.
  • Rare materials the guild's expeditions bring back, like ebony logs and adaman ore, sold only for Everglass, plus Supply Crates of crafting materials at the guild hall.
  • The Rewind Hourglass: after a knockout, buy back the experience it cost, or return to where you fell.
  • Cash out a little for gil each day.

The guild feast

Anyone can treat the whole server: +10% experience and better skill-ups for everyone online, for an hour.

Guild projects

The server funds shared goals together: a Grand Feast, a Champion Season where everyone has a lure, or a Rift Surge that doubles Time Rifts.

!project

The Wandering Merchant

Odilo turns up every few hours in Jeuno and the three nations with rare stock: Reraisers, elixirs, Icarus Wings, rare materials and furniture.

!merchant
