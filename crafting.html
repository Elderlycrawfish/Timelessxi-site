Craft & Fortune

Craft & Fortune

Some legends are forged. Some are caught.

A flash of insight at the anvil. A rich vein that moves with the day. A whisper of a fish so old it has a name. Every skill pays its way here, and some pay in stories.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026

At the workbench

Every synth counts

Sometimes the hammer falls just right, and the whole server hears about it.

Crafting levels faster and rewards you for coming back to it. Time away banks rested synths. A broken synth teaches you something. Make a recipe enough times and you master it.

!craft · /masterworks

  • Flash of Insight: a rare moment of brilliance, a sure skill-up or a near-certain high quality.
  • Masterworks: master a recipe and chase a synth that bursts gold.
  • Daily orders matched to your skill.

Into the wild

Gathering

Logging, mining, harvesting and excavation each level to 100. A rich spot moves every day. Find it first and the server hears your name.

!gather

Chocobo digging

Treasure maps, rich veins, jackpots and a chocobo who digs better the more you raise it.

!dig

Chocobo raising

Raise a chick to an adult in about a week, and ride one faster than any rental.

!chocobo

Timeless Fishing

There are eight fish in Vana'diel with names

Fishermen speak of them quietly.

Every cast plays Timeless Fishing, a skill game where you steer the catch zone to keep the fish in it. No empty catches, no guessing at arrows. Perfect catches pay off.

And somewhere out there are the Elders of the Deep. Land one and it swims in your Timeless Aquarium, a gilded show piece that lights up with every legend you add. Coming soon.

  • Ser Barbel the Unbowed
  • The Ore-Eater
  • Whiskerwise
  • The Crimson Admiral
  • Moonfin
  • Rimecoil
  • Herald of Tavnazia
  • Siren's Lantern

Everglass

The guild pays in light

The Artisans Guild runs on Everglass, earned from expeditions, bounties, requests and Time Rifts. Spend it on what moves you forward.

The guild feast

One generous adventurer can treat the whole server to bonus experience for an hour.

Guild projects

The server funds shared goals together: a Grand Feast, a Champion Season, a Rift Surge.

!project

The Wandering Merchant

Odilo turns up every few hours with rare stock. Where is he now?

!merchant

The Goblin Bazaar

Every other night, a goblin pitches camp somewhere in the wilds. The server hears which zone, never where. First to find him wins a prize.

!bazaar
