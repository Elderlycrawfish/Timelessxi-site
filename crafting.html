Craft & Fortune

Craft & Fortune

Some legends are forged. Some are caught.

A flash of insight at the anvil. A rich vein that moves with the day. A whisper of a fish so old it has a name. Every skill pays its way here, and some pay in stories.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026

At the workbench

Every synth counts

Sometimes the hammer falls just right, and the whole server hears about it.

Crafting levels faster and rewards you for coming back to it. Time away banks rested synths. A broken synth teaches you something. Make a recipe enough times and you master it.

  • Flash of Insight: a rare moment of brilliance
  • Masterworks: chase a synth that bursts gold
  • Artisan storage for your whole account: craft straight from it
  • Daily orders matched to your skill

!craft · /masterworks

Into the wild

The ground has more to give

Today's rich vein is somewhere in this zone. Find it first.

Logging, mining, harvesting and excavation each level to 100. Your chocobo digs up treasure maps and jackpots. Raise a chick to a full-grown chocobo in about a week.

!gather · !dig · !chocobo

Timeless Fishing

There are eight fish in Vana'diel with names

Fishermen speak of them quietly.

Every cast plays Timeless Fishing, a skill game where you steer to keep the fish in your catch zone. No empty catches, no guessing at arrows.

Somewhere out there are the Elders of the Deep. Land one and it swims in your Timeless Aquarium, a gilded show piece that lights up with every legend you add. Coming soon.

  • Ser Barbel the Unbowed
  • The Ore-Eater
  • Whiskerwise
  • The Crimson Admiral
  • Moonfin
  • Rimecoil
  • Herald of Tavnazia
  • Siren's Lantern

Everglass

The guild pays in light

A goblin is camped somewhere in the wilds tonight. The server knows which zone. Nobody knows where.

The Artisans Guild runs on Everglass, earned from expeditions, bounties, requests and Time Rifts. Treat the whole server to a feast, fund a Rift Surge, or hunt down the Goblin Bazaar before dawn.

!shop · !bazaar · !merchant
