Feature 4 of 6

Every hour pays off

Treasure worth hunting for.

Your gil and gear come from playing, not from hours of farming on the side. Crafting, gathering and fishing level faster, and everything you do feeds a guild economy with its own currency.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Treasure Caskets

Caskets you'll go out looking for

Every monster that gives experience can leave a Treasure Casket. Touch it and the contents go straight into your bags, with no guessing game. Every pull is a shot at your next upgrade, so hunting is how you gear up and fund your adventures.

Blue casket

Sums of gil scaled to the monster's level, plus crafting materials for your skill range. Now and then, a piece of gear.

Brown casket

A piece of gear within a few levels of the monster: weapons and armor from across the era.

Rainbow casket

The rare one: an upgrade chosen for your own job and level.

Everglass

The server's own currency

Earn Everglass by supplying the guild's expeditions, filling guild requests and craft orders, finishing bounties and closing Time Rifts. Then spend it on what moves you forward.

  • The Everglass Shop (!shop): Champion lures, gatherer's focus, rested synths, the guilds' crafting gear, chocobo treats and extra companion slots.
  • Vault rare finds from the expeditions, and supply crates of crafting materials.
  • The Rewind Hourglass: after a knockout, buy back the experience it cost, or return to where you fell.
  • Cash out a little for gil each day.

The guild feast

Anyone can treat the whole server: +10% experience and better skill-ups for everyone online, for an hour.

Guild projects

The server funds shared goals together: a Grand Feast, a Champion Season where everyone has a lure, or a Rift Surge that doubles Time Rifts.

!project

The Wandering Merchant

Odilo turns up every few hours in Jeuno and the three nations with rare stock: Reraisers, elixirs, Icarus Wings, rare materials and furniture.

!merchant

Crafting, reforged

Every synth counts

Rested synths

Every hour away from crafting banks 2 rested synths, up to 50, each with better skill-up odds.

Flash of Insight

A 2% chance on any synth of a big skill-up or a high-quality boost.

Lessons and mastery

A broken synth teaches you something; making the same recipe 25, 50 and 100 times raises its high-quality rate.

Craft orders and the craftbook

Three daily orders in your range for gil, standing and rested synths. /craft shows recipes, what you can make now and auction prices.

Gathering, digging and fishing

ActivityWhat's new
GatheringLogging, mining, harvesting and excavation each level 1 to 100: fewer broken tools, more and rarer finds, and a rich spot that moves every day. !gather
Chocobo diggingSix times faster levelling and 250 finds a day, plus treasure maps, rich veins, jackpots and daily requests. !dig
Chocobo raisingFour times faster than retail: a raising day is 6 real hours, SS ranks are possible, and a raised chocobo rides as fast as a rental or faster. !chocobo
Timeless FishingA skill-based mini-game replaces the arrow game: no empty catches, extra fish from easy waters, and perfect catches pay off.

The Artisans Guild

Lower Jeuno

AI crafters

Four crafters turn what players sell to vendors into goods on the auction house, and keep ammo, ninja tools and food in stock.

Commissions and requests

Bring ingredients and a fee for a craft that never fails. Every 3 hours each crafter posts requests that pay above NPC prices.

Gathering expeditions

A guild crew is always out in a beastman land for 2 hours. Donate gear, food or gil, or hunt beastmen there, and earn Everglass.

!guild · !expedition
