Getting Started · First Steps

Back to Vana'diel in six steps

From download to your first party.

All you need is a Windows PC. The Timeless XI setup installs the Timeless XI client and finds or installs FINAL FANTASY XI for you.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026
  1. 1

    Join the Discord

    Announcements, patch notes and help all live there, so it's the best first stop.

    Join the Discord
  2. 2

    Read the server rules

    Our rules are short, and they keep Timeless XI fair, friendly and fun for everyone. Please take a few minutes to read them before you create your account: when you make one, you're agreeing to play by them, and knowing them up front helps you start on the right foot.

    Read the rules
  3. 3

    Download the Timeless XI setup

    One download sets up everything. The setup finds FINAL FANTASY XI on your PC, or downloads it from Square Enix for you, then installs the Timeless XI client and every Timeless patch. Have about 28 GB free.

    Download available at launch

    The setup will be posted here and in the Discord when Timeless XI opens. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for a new program: choose More info, then Run anyway.

  4. 4

    Create your account

    Open Timeless XI and choose Create Account on the login screen (creating one means you agree to the server rules). After that, just Log In, or tick Stay logged in and the client takes you straight to your characters.

    The same account logs you in to My Account on this site.

  5. 5

    Create your character

    Choose Create Character, then pick your race, face, size, job and nation while your new adventurer turns in front of you.

  6. 6

    Step into Vana'diel

    Say hello in the Timeless linkshell, then head outside the city gates and type !companion to call a full party. The New Player Guide walks you from level 1 to 75.

    New Player Guide

Your first commands

Type these in the chat box once you're in.

!companion
Call a full party of companions
/cmgr
Companion Manager window
/tm
The Timeless menu: every built-in tool
!bounty
Today's bounties
!chores
Your moogle's two daily chores
!shop
Spend Everglass
!journal
Your quests and what to do next

Mods on Timeless

The Timeless XI client installs and updates the approved mods for you, from the NM Atlas to the Companion Manager. Find them all behind the hourglass button.

See the mod rules →

Need a hand?

  • Update Required? The client checks for a new build each time it starts. Choose Update and it restarts on the new one.
  • Settings and hotbars are saved on your PC, per character; your characters live on the server.
  • Not sure what's allowed? Read the server rules.
  • Still stuck? Ask in the Discord's help channel.
