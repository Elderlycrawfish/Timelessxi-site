Getting Started · First Steps

Back to Vana'diel in six steps

From download to your first party.

All you need is a Windows PC. The Timeless XI installer sets up the launcher and finds or installs FINAL FANTASY XI for you.

COMING SOONDecember 2026
  1. 1

    Join the Discord

    Announcements, patch notes and help all live there, so it's the best first stop.

    Join the Discord
  2. 2

    Read the server rules

    Our rules are short, and they keep Timeless XI fair, friendly and fun for everyone. Please take a few minutes to read them before you create your account: when you make one, you're agreeing to play by them, and knowing them up front helps you start on the right foot.

    Read the rules
  3. 3

    Run the Timeless XI installer

    Download the Timeless XI installer and run it. It installs the Timeless XI Launcher and checks for FINAL FANTASY XI. Don't have the game? The installer can download Square Enix's official installer for you (about 7 GB), or you can point it at a copy you already have.

    Download available at launch

    The installer will be posted here and in the Discord when Timeless XI opens in December 2026. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for a new program: choose More info, then Run anyway.

  4. 4

    Let the game update

    Installed FINAL FANTASY XI fresh? Open PlayOnline once and let the game finish updating; the installer's last page has a button for it. Already up to date? Skip ahead.

  5. 5

    Press Update, then Play

    The launcher's main button reads Update whenever something new is ready: Timeless XI game files, a newer launcher, or a setup fix. Click it, allow Windows' prompt once, and it turns into Play. The first time, choose to create a new account in the login window; after that, just sign in.

    The launcher also keeps your macros and settings saving properly, and backs them up before each session.

  6. 6

    Step into Vana'diel

    Say hello in the Timeless linkshell, then head outside the city gates and type !companion to call a full party. The New Player Guide walks you from level 1 to 75.

    New Player Guide

Your first commands

!bounty

!chores
Your moogle's two daily chores
!shop

!journal

Quality-of-life add-ons come with the launcher. Ones that give an unfair edge are marked there and can't be switched on.

Need a hand?

  • Macros not saving? Use Maintenance › Fix saving in the launcher.
  • Wrong resolution? Set it on the launcher's Graphics tab, not in the game's config tool.
  • Not sure what's allowed? Read the server rules.
  • Still stuck? Ask in the Discord's help channel.
