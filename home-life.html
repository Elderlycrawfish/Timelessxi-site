Enhancement 5 of 6

A home worth coming back to

Your Mog House works for you.

The furniture you place powers bonuses everywhere you go. Your moogle has small daily chores, your garden levels up and grows rare crops, and every login starts with a short summary of what happened while you were away.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

The Hearth

Furniture with a purpose

Each piece adds points to its element, and pieces crafters can make count double. At 12, 30 and 60 points an element reaches tiers 1, 2 and 3.

Harmonious Home

Bring every element to tier 1 and each one counts a tier higher.

Furniture sets

Eight sets each add a perk when complete, like an extra Hunter's Mark per bounty or longer Champion lures.

!hearth · !hearth sets · /craft mog

Daily life

Moogle chores

Two small chores a day, from a few synths to a bounty or a harvest. Each pays Mog Tokens for rare furniture, seeds, flowerpots, chocobo treats and rested synths.

!chores · !moogle

Garden Plus

Gardening levels 1 to 100: more crops, a chance to get your seeds back, and plants that take longer to wilt. The right plant, crystal and pot can crossbreed a rare crop.

!garden pots · !garden crosses

Welcome back

One login summary of everything that happened while you were away: errands, commissions, your chocobo, your garden, the expedition, bounties and more.
