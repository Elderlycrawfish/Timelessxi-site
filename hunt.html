The Hunt

Every pull could be the one.

Any monster might be a Champion, enhanced Treasure Caskets open with a single click, and daily bounties give every session a goal. Hunting is how you gear up and fund your adventures.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Champion monsters

Catch them all, if you can

Any monster you meet could turn out to be a Champion: tougher, stranger, and carrying far better rewards. Every hunt is a chance to find one you have never faced, and to collect every trait in your Bestiary.

RankStrengthTraitsRewards
ChampionTwice the HP1, or 2 from level 50Bonus EXP, gil, materials and trophies
EliteThree times the HP2Double rewards, and the whole zone hears of it
LegendaryFour times the HP3Five times the rewards, often rare furniture, and the whole server hears of it

13 traits, endless surprises

Is it Vampiric, Shielded, Blinking or Alpha? A Champion's name hints at what you're in for, and every trait changes the fight: one shrugs off weapons, another calls its pack. Stay ready, read the signs, and adapt your plan on the fly.

Size up your target: !champions look

Trophies and the Bestiary

Every Champion pays trophies for each of its traits. Trade them for clusters, materials, lures, rare furniture and themed rewards. Your Bestiary turns kills into permanent damage bonuses against each trait.

!trophies · /craft champions

Champion lures

A lure makes Champions six times as common for 30 minutes: about 1 in 33. Buy one with Everglass, Hunter's Marks or trophies.

Treasure Caskets

Treasure worth hunting for

Every monster that gives experience can leave a Treasure Casket. Touch it and the contents go straight into your bags, with no guessing game. Every pull is a shot at your next upgrade, so hunting is how you gear up and fund your adventures.

Blue casket

Sums of gil scaled to the monster's level, plus crafting materials for your skill range. Now and then, a piece of gear.

Brown casket

A piece of gear within a few levels of the monster: weapons and armor from across the era.

Rainbow casket

The rare one: an upgrade chosen for your own job and level.

Records of Eminence, reimagined

No more hunting for a Fields or Grounds of Valor book: pick a training page from the Records of Eminence menu anywhere and spend your time on EXP, not running. 448 repeatable pages by zone and level pay gil, tabs and EXP.

Hunting notorious monsters? Every one is on a set timer. See them all in the NM Tracker.
