The Hunt

Every pull could be the one.

Any monster might be a Champion, Treasure Caskets open with a single click, the beastmen push back when they win the war, and every monster family has a card to collect. Hunting is how you gear up and fund your adventures.

COMING SOON

Champion monsters

Catch them all, if you can

Any monster you meet could turn out to be a Champion: tougher, stranger, and carrying far better rewards. Every hunt is a chance to find one you have never faced, and to collect every trait in your Bestiary.

RankStrengthTraitsRewards
ChampionTwice the HP1, or 2 from level 50Bonus EXP, gil, materials and trophies
EliteThree times the HP2Double rewards, and the whole zone hears of it
LegendaryFour times the HP3Five times the rewards, often rare furniture, and the whole server hears of it

13 traits, endless surprises

Is it Vampiric, Shielded, Blinking or Alpha? A Champion's name hints at what you're in for, and every trait changes the fight: one shrugs off weapons, another calls its pack. Stay ready, read the signs, and adapt your plan on the fly.

Size up your target: !champions look

Trophies and the Bestiary

Every Champion pays trophies for each of its traits. Trade them for clusters, materials, lures, rare furniture and themed rewards. Your Bestiary turns kills into permanent damage bonuses against each trait.

!trophies

Champion lures

A lure makes Champions six times as common for 30 minutes: about 1 in 33. Buy one with Everglass, Hunter's Marks or trophies.

Everglass Shop · Bounty Board

The Bounty Board

New hunts every day and week

Daily and weekly bounties

Three new hunts every day and two every week: Champions, notorious monsters, a monster family or the guild expedition's foes. Finish all three dailies for a bonus.

!bounty

Hunter's Marks

Bounties pay Everglass, guild standing and Hunter's Marks. Marks buy Champion lures, or a clue that makes your next notorious monster drop a lost guild recipe.

The world fights back

Conquest, the moon and the days

Beastman warbands

Let the beastmen win a region and warbands gather in its fields, led by an Elite Champion captain. Drive them off for conquest points and Everglass.

A war you can win

Conquest built for a close-knit server: regions keep half their influence each week, garrisons patrol on their own, and every kill counts for your nation, Signet or not.

Moon and night

Under a full moon the beastmen rally and Champions grow common. On Darksday nights the undead rise. On moonless nights, Fomor bands stalk Misareaux and Lufaise.

Rally calls

When Fafnir, King Behemoth or another great notorious monster appears, the whole server hears about it.

Treasure Caskets

Treasure worth hunting for

Every monster that gives experience can leave a Treasure Casket. Touch it and the contents go straight into your bags, with no guessing game. Every pull is a shot at your next upgrade, so hunting is how you gear up and fund your adventures.

Blue casket

Sums of gil scaled to the monster's level, plus crafting materials for your skill range. Now and then, a piece of gear.

Brown casket

A piece of gear within a few levels of the monster: weapons and armor from across the era.

Rainbow casket

The rare one: an upgrade chosen for your own job and level.

Cardian

Vana’diel’s own card game

Every monster family has a card, and the hunt is how you collect them. Play on a board of three real regions, where the day, the weather and the moon shift the numbers.

162 cards to find: commons from the field, rare cards from notorious monsters, and shining foils from Champions. Some only turn up at night, in a storm, or while the beastmen hold the land.

  • Pull up a chair with the regulars at the Steaming Sheep after dark, and beat them for their own cards.
  • Chase Shaali Nine-Lives, the travelling card shark, from town to town for notorious monsters’ cards.
  • Uncover the Card Club, a secret circle of card players hiding in plain sight.
  • Challenge another player, or play for a card and put your best one on the line.
  • Complete a set for its crest card and a purse of gil.

Records of Eminence, reimagined

No more hunting for a Fields or Grounds of Valor book: pick a training page from the Records of Eminence menu anywhere and spend your time on EXP, not running. 448 repeatable pages by zone and level pay gil, tabs and EXP.

Hunting notorious monsters? Every one is on a set timer. See them all in the NM Tracker.
