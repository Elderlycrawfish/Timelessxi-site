The Hunt

The Hunt

Something out there is wearing your next upgrade.

One monster in two hundred isn't what it seems. Any kill might leave treasure behind. And the notorious monsters of old come back on their own schedule, no camping required.

Champion monsters

Not every Goblin is just a Goblin

A Vampiric Goblin Smithy, healing with every swing. A Shielded Orc that laughs at your sword. A Legendary one the whole server hears about.

Any monster might be a Champion: tougher, stranger, and carrying far better rewards. Thirteen traits change how each fight plays, and three ranks decide how much trouble you're in.

  • Read the signs with !champions look
  • Every trait you defeat makes you stronger against it, for good
  • Lures make them six times as common

Treasure Caskets

Treasure that finds you

Any monster worth experience can leave a casket. One touch and it's yours. No guessing game, no traps.

Blue

Gil, crafting materials, and now and then a piece of gear.

Brown

Gear from across the era, near the monster's level.

Rainbow

The rare one. An upgrade chosen for your own job.

Notorious monsters

No more lottery camps

When it's time, it's back.

Every notorious monster returns on its own timer. No placeholders, no hours of killing the wrong lizard. And when Fafnir or King Behemoth wakes, a rally call goes out to the whole server.
