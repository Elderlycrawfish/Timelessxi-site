Always something to hunt

Every pull could be the one.

Any monster might be a Champion. A rift might tear open beside you, or a grain of Eternal Sand might carry your party twenty years into the past. Daily bounties give every session a goal.

Champion monsters

About 1 monster in 200 is a Champion

It's decided the first time someone fights it, and only for monsters worth experience to you. Its name shows its traits: "Vampiric Goblin Smithy".

13 traits to learn

Hasted, Shielded, Warded, Frenzied, Vampiric, Venomous, Enraged, Thorned, Renewing, Undying, Elemental, Blinking and Alpha. Shielded shrugs off weapons, Warded shrugs off magic, Alpha calls its pack: every one needs a different plan.

Trophies and the Bestiary

Every Champion pays trophies for each of its traits. Trade them for clusters, materials, lures, rare furniture and themed rewards. Your Bestiary turns kills into permanent damage bonuses against each trait.

!trophies · /craft champions

Champion lures

A lure makes Champions six times as common for 30 minutes: about 1 in 33. Buy one with Everglass, Hunter's Marks or trophies.

Time Rifts

Classic notorious monsters, echoed

Every few minutes a rift might tear open near an adventurer in the field. Step through with your party into a private arena, where an echo of a classic notorious monster waits, rebuilt at your level, with one twist.

Echoes include Leaping Lizzy, Valkurm Emperor, Golden Bat, Argus, Nue, Roc, Simurgh, Serket, Voluptuous Vivian and more. Beat it in 15 minutes for Everglass, experience and the echo's own drops.

  • Hastened: it attacks much faster
  • Twin Echo: two of them, both must fall
  • Rewind: once, at 25% HP, it recovers to 60%
  • Time Drag: everyone inside is slowed every 40 seconds
  • Accelerating: it grows stronger every minute
  • Swift Sands: only 8 minutes, but 1.5x rewards
Ancient Rifts: collect echo shards, then open a rift where your party fights a legend for its own loot

Eternal Sand

Step into the past

Monsters in 18 zones with a past version can drop Eternal Sand. Choose on the spot: step into the past with your party, or let it scatter. In the past, nearby monsters rise to your level + 6 and experience is 1.5x.

Beastman Warlords

An Elite Champion warlord of the zone's tribe can take the field: Orc, Quadav, Yagudo or the Shadow Lord's army. Beat one for 25 Everglass and 5 more minutes in the past. Beat all four tribes to become Warlord Slayer.

Last Grains

The final 2 minutes of every visit burn: experience triples and monsters fight harder.

The Sands Converge

When 20 sands are used across the server in one day, a one-hour event begins: sand drops twice as often and every visit gets a warlord.

Notorious monsters and training

Timed notorious monsters

219 lottery NMs spawn on a timer instead of from placeholders. No more killing placeholders for hours hoping for a pop: when the timer is up, the NM appears. Time-of-day and weather rules still apply.

See every timer in the NM Tracker

Records of Eminence, reimagined

No more hunting for a Fields or Grounds of Valor book: pick a training page from the Records of Eminence menu anywhere and spend your time on EXP, not running. 448 repeatable pages by zone and level pay gil, tabs and EXP.
