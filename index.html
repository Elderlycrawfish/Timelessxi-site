The classic you love, carefully reforged.

The Vana'diel you know and love, from Rise of the Zilart through Treasures of Aht Urhgan, carefully reforged. Quality-of-life improvements like fast home point travel smooth out the old frustrations, every job has been reworked with new mechanics and a signature quest, and highly intelligent AI companions fill out your party and play their jobs like seasoned adventurers. Crafting and gathering level faster and pay off more, Champion monsters bring new challenges to the field, and high-value notorious monsters are within reach without the endless lottery camps.

Classic at heart

Rooted in the era you love.

Timeless is for players who still remember their first party in the Valkurm Dunes, the thrill of a well-timed skillchain, and the satisfaction of a great EXP session with friends. We didn't replace that game; we built on it. Plenty is new, but the spirit of the original remains at the heart of everything.

What stays classic

  • ◇The level 75 cap, and the climb to get there
  • ◇Vana'diel itself: the original areas, Rise of the Zilart, Chains of Promathia and Treasures of Aht Urhgan, with their timeless stories and nostalgia
  • ◇Each job's classic role: the new mechanics deepen it, they don't replace it
  • ◇The sights and sounds you remember, from the music of Ronfaure to the bustle of Jeuno

Reforged, top to bottom

Deeper jobs. A busier world. A party whenever you need one.

Every job has new mechanics and a quest for its own signature weapon. Out in the field, any monster might turn out to be a Champion, and the Bounty Board always has a hunt posted. Crafting, gathering and digging level far faster and feed a guild that pays in grubstakes. Your Mog House furniture and garden power bonuses you carry everywhere. And with AI companions, you never wait on a full party to see any of it.

Reforged features

Adventure

How you fight, who you fight with, and what's waiting out there.

Every job, reworked

All 18 jobs gain new mechanics: Warrior weapon mastery, Monk Ki, White Mage Miracles, Black Mage Overloads, Samurai Iaijutsu and more. Each has a quest line for a signature Rare/Ex weapon.

See every job below

Companions

Call AI recruits who play like real players of their job: they tank, heal, skillchain and magic burst. They grow stronger as your bond rises, and call out tips when a Champion appears.

/cm · /cv · !companion

Champions and bounties

About 1 monster in 200 is a Champion, and the rarest are Elite or Legendary, with up to four times the HP and three traits. Earn trophies, fill your Bestiary, and take daily and weekly bounties.

!champions · !trophies · !bounty

Records of Eminence, reimagined

448 repeatable training pages, sorted by zone and level across the home nations, the Outlands and Aht Urhgan. Hunt a monster type for gil, tabs and EXP.

Open the Records of Eminence menu

Making a living

Crafting, gathering and a guild economy that rewards every hour you put in.

Crafting, reforged

Rested synths, a Flash of Insight, lessons from broken synths and recipe mastery speed every craft along. Three daily craft orders, and the craftbook shows what you can make and what it sells for.

/craft · !craft orders

Gathering, digging and chocobos

Logging, mining, harvesting and excavation each level from 1 to 100, with a rich spot that moves every day. Chocobo digging levels six times faster, and raising your own chocobo is four times faster than retail.

!gather · !dig · !chocobo

The Artisans Guild

Four AI crafters in Lower Jeuno take commissions, post material requests and stock the auction house. Their crew is always out on a gathering expedition you can supply.

!guild · !expedition

Grubstakes and the shop

The server's own currency, from the guild, expeditions and bounties. Spend it on vault rare finds, supply crates, extra companion slots, Champion lures, a server-wide feast, or cash it out for gil.

!shop

Home and comfort

A Mog House worth coming back to, better treasure, and fewer old headaches.

Your Mog House, empowered

Every piece of furniture adds to its element, and each element boosts something you carry everywhere: rested synths, digging and gathering experience, high-quality synths, companion bond, even Champion chance.

!hearth · /craft mog

Gardening and moogle chores

Gardening has its own levels and rare crossbreed crops. Your moogle asks for two small chores a day, paying Mog Tokens for rare furniture, seeds and treats.

!garden · !chores · !moogle

Treasure Caskets, rebuilt

Any monster that gives EXP can leave a casket. Blue holds gil and crafting materials, brown holds gear, and the rare rainbow casket holds an upgrade for your own job and level.

Every job, reworked

Your job, with new tricks

Each job keeps its classic identity and gains mechanics that reward playing it well, plus a quest for a Rare/Ex weapon only that job can earn. Recruits learn the new tricks too.

WAR
Warrior Weapon Mastery perks and NM trophies for every weapon type; Never Yield keeps you standing. Quest reward: Unbroken
MNK
Monk Ki builds with every blow; five Pressure Points turn your next weapon skill into Dim Mak. Quest reward: Iron Lotus
WHM
White Mage Overhealing becomes Regen, and Faith builds toward a Miracle cure, Raise or Banish. Quest reward: Pilgrim's Mercy
BLM
Black Mage Attune to an element, then release it as an Overload. Spell Echo and ancient magic effects. Quest reward: Unwritten Flame
RDM
Red Mage Duelist's Rhythm weaves spells and blades; En Garde adds your enspell to every weapon skill hit. Quest reward: Crimson Duelist
THF
Thief Honor Amongst Thieves: more gil and Treasure Hunter when Thieves hunt together. Quest reward: Calling Card
PLD
Paladin A great sword damage stance with Oathblade weapon skills, plus Cover on recruits. Quest reward: Oathsworn Blade
DRK
Dark Knight Reaper's Art scythe skills and Soul Feast, or the Abyssal Guard tanking road. Quest reward: Reaper's Due · Abyssal Vigil
BST
Beastmaster Blood Scent marks your prey, and Wild Bond makes your pet stronger with every worthy kill. Quest reward: Pridewarden
BRD
Bard Crescendo strengthens re-sung songs, and Encore sings every fifth song twice as fast. Quest reward: Corvin's Flute · Final Encore
RNG
Ranger Haste speeds up ranged attacks, Mark Prey grows your damage, and sweet-spot hits build to a sure critical. Quest reward: Great Hunt
SAM
Samurai Iaijutsu opening strikes, a growing Hasso, and Bond of Blades: party TP for every skillchain you close. Quest reward: Mushin-maru
NIN
Ninja Kawarimi grants free shadows, and the Elemental Wheel stacks ninjutsu damage. Quest reward: Yamikaze · Kirigakure
DRG
Dragoon Shorter Jump recasts, and Bonds of the Wyvern: breaths that burst into skillchains. Quest reward: Last Dragoon
SMN
Summoner Pact Bond levels up each avatar, and Pact Weaving chains one Blood Pact into the next avatar. Quest reward: Ruby Covenant
BLU
Blue Mage Beast Essence builds to Unleash: 30 seconds of stronger, free blue magic. Quest reward: Azure Oath
COR
Corsair Hot Streak protects lucky rolls, and Powder Keg makes Quick Draw hit hard. Quest reward: Luzaf's Wager · Marzaha's Ladybug
PUP
Puppetmaster Dancing on Strings combos you and your automaton, and Steam Vent turns overloads into damage. Quest reward: Stringmaster
