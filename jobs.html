New abilities, job by job

Main-job Warriors through Puppetmasters

Here is what each job gains on Timeless XI. Your companions play the same jobs, so they use these tricks too.

Warrior

WAR
  • Weapon Mastery: a perk for the weapon in hand: Double Attack with a great axe, crit rate with an axe, accuracy with a sword, Store TP with a club, and more.
  • Weapon Trophies: every NM you finish counts for your weapon type; at 1, 5, 15 and 30 you gain up to +8 accuracy and attack with it.
  • Rallying Warcry: Warcry also gives everyone it reaches +10 accuracy for 30 seconds.
  • Defender and Provoke: Defender keeps more enmity, blocks more often and gives TP per block; Provoke adds extra lasting enmity under Defender.
  • Berserk: grows stronger as your Warrior levels up, reaching +35% attack.

Monk

MNK
  • Ki: every hit, kick and counter builds Ki; spend it on a stronger Chakra, Chi Blast, or a +25% weapon skill at 100.
  • Pressure Points: crits and kicks mark the target; at 5 marks your next weapon skill strikes a vital point: +30% and a short stun (Dim Mak).
  • Martial Forms: Focus, Dodge and Counterstance add Tiger, Crane and Mountain forms: crit damage, counters, less damage taken.
  • Empty Hand: fight with nothing in the main hand for +20 attack, more kicks and Store TP.
  • Ability tune-ups: each Boost adds 20% attack and still stacks, Chakra also cures ailments and adds Regen, Chi Blast dispels, and Dodge raises your guard.

White Mage

WHM
  • Grace: healing past max HP isn't wasted: the overflow becomes a Regen on your target.
  • Faith: healing and cleansing others builds Faith; at 100 your next spell is a Miracle: a party-wide Cure, an instant Raise or a double-damage Banish.
  • Purifying Light: there's no Esuna in this era, so Cures have a chance to remove Poison, Paralysis, Blindness or Silence.
  • Blessed Benediction: after "The Long Night", Benediction also grants Regen and Protect.

Black Mage

BLM
  • Elemental Attunement: chain the same element for up to +15%; switch to the opposite element to release it all as an Overload.
  • Spell Echo: a nuke can echo for an extra 25% of its damage (more often under Elemental Seal).
  • Forbidden Knowledge: Ancient Magic leaves something behind: Flare burns, Freeze binds, Burst stuns, Flood drowns.
  • Last Ember: below 25% MP your nukes hit 15% harder and black magic costs half.
  • Magic bursts for everyone: bursts on regular monsters get +35 magic accuracy and extra damage, for every job.

Red Mage

RDM
  • Duelist's Rhythm: after a spell your melee hits harder; after a melee round your spells cast faster. Weave them.
  • Exploit Weakness: every enfeeble on your target adds +3% to your melee, enspells and nukes, up to +15%.
  • En Garde: a weapon skill right after an enfeeble adds your enspell damage to every hit.
  • Dual Hex: an enfeeble can bring a lighter second one with it.
  • Crimson Surge: after Convert, your next three spells are free and fast.

Thief

THF
  • Honor Amongst Thieves: more Thieves in the alliance means more gil, higher Treasure Hunter and bonus crits from behind.
  • Assassin's Art: dagger weapon skills deal +25%.
  • From the Shadows: Hide readies a free Sneak Attack, and your first round out of hiding adds no enmity.
  • Steal Their Edge and Pickpocket: a Steal also saps the monster's STR, DEX or AGI; an empty Steal lifts gil instead.
  • Poisoned Blades: dagger hits can poison, and Thieves deal 5% more to poisoned monsters. Mug adds +5% crit rate for everyone.

Paladin

PLD
  • Crusader stance: a great sword stance from "Oath of the Two-Handed Blade": attack, Double Attack and Enlight damage.
  • Holy Circle: also raises the party's damage against the undead.
  • Cover: usable on alliance members and companions.
  • Better shields: +5% block rate, and blocked hits are softened by 10% more.
  • Faster Chivalry: learned at 45, and merits shorten its recast.

Dark Knight

DRK
  • Abyssal Guard: a great sword tanking stance from "Nightfall".
  • Reaper's Art: scythe weapon skills deal +25%, and scythe crits drain HP (Reaper's Harvest).
  • Soul Feast: a killing blow under Souleater returns 5% HP and 50 TP.
  • Chain Reaper: quick kills stack attack up to +10%.
  • Stronger Absorbs: Absorb spells last longer, and Dark Seal makes them 50% stronger. Arcane Circle raises damage against arcana.

Beastmaster

BST
  • Blood Scent: your pet's special moves mark the monster: +10% melee for you and your pet, +25% on your weapon skills.
  • Wild Bond: every worthy kill with the same pet raises its bond, up to +15% attack.
  • Feral Fury: axe weapon skills deal +25%.
  • Pack Leader: your pet deals +15% while you fight the same monster.
  • Treats: Reward also gives your pet Haste and TP. Jug pets now get proper ranged accuracy and attack.

Bard

BRD
  • Crescendo: re-sing a song still on someone and it comes back 10% stronger, up to +30%.
  • Encore: every 5th song in a fight comes out twice as fast.
  • Battle Hymn: your dagger and sword hits extend your songs on the party.
  • Soul Voice: every song also grants Regen and Refresh, and Lullaby and Elegy can't be resisted.
  • Campfire Tales: party members resting under your Ballad or Paeon recover 50% more. Threnody also boosts its element's damage.

Ranger

RNG
  • Mark Prey: the longer a monster is your prey, the harder your shots hit, up to +10%.
  • Focus: sweet-spot hits build Focus; at 5, your next shot is a guaranteed critical hit.
  • Camouflage ambush: the first shot out of Camouflage deals +50% and adds no enmity.
  • Steady Aim: well-placed shots add less enmity.
  • Eagle Eye Shot and Scavenge: Eagle Eye Shot always hits, and Scavenge recovers the ammo you fired.

Samurai

SAM
  • Iaijutsu: a weapon skill that opens the fight deals +25%, and Meditate readies another draw.
  • Kenjutsu: great katana weapon skills deal +30%.
  • Growing Hasso: attack builds every round you stay in Hasso, up to +15%, and Hasso gives more haste.
  • Seigan: every attack turned aside by Third Eye or countered gives 50 TP.
  • Bond of Blades: closing a skillchain gives your party 100 TP.

Ninja

NIN
  • Kawarimi: when your last shadow breaks, a log can take the next hit.
  • Shadow Weave: every shadow that takes a hit gives 15 TP.
  • Elemental Wheel: cast elemental ninjutsu in wheel order to chain up to +30%, and +50% on the sixth.
  • Shadow Step and From Smoke: build stacks from behind under Innin for a +30% weapon skill; the first round after Tonko is all crits.
  • Yonin: no longer decays, and adds counters, enmity and faster Utsusemi. Ninjutsu gains magic accuracy and bursts.

Dragoon

DRG
  • Wyvern's Fury: if your wyvern falls, grief turns to +15% attack and crit rate.
  • Bonded Pair: every minute in combat together builds accuracy and attack for you and your wyvern.
  • Breath Burst: your wyvern breathes the element that bursts your party's skillchains, for +50%.
  • Aerial Combo: Jumps and weapon skills boost each other when chained.
  • Ancient Circle: raises the party's damage against dragons. Shorter Jump recasts.

Summoner

SMN
  • Pact Bond: each avatar levels up with you: accuracy, Double Attack, stronger Blood Pacts and cheaper perpetuation.
  • Pact Weaving: release your avatar after a Blood Pact and its spirit lingers to strike again; summon the next one to chain it.
  • Mana Tether and Pact Siphon: high Summoning skill cuts perpetuation cost, and landed Blood Pacts return 5% of their damage as MP.
  • Carbuncle's Grace: while Carbuncle is out, everyone nearby slowly recovers HP and MP.

Blue Mage

BLU
  • Beast Essence: every blue spell, hit and weapon skill builds Essence for stronger physical blue magic.
  • Unleash: at 100 Essence the beast breaks loose: +20% damage and free blue magic for 30 seconds.
  • Fresh Memory: when a monster uses a move you know, your next cast of it deals +25%.
  • Azure Chain and Spell Weaving: a physical spell under Chain Affinity makes your next magical spell burst for +35%, and alternating physical and magical spells builds up to +15%.

Corsair

COR
  • Hot Streak: lucky rolls protect your next Double-Up; three in a row give your party 100 TP.
  • Cutlass and Pistol: gun shots and sword or dagger weapon skills boost each other when chained.
  • Powder Keg: Quick Draw on a monster carrying its element's debuff deals +50% and renews it.
  • All Hands on Deck: when a party member carrying two of your rolls lands a weapon skill, you gain 20 TP.
  • Pirate's Take: finishing a monster with Quick Draw or a ranged weapon skill can pay out extra gil.

Puppetmaster

PUP
  • Dancing on Strings: your weapon skill and your automaton's boost each other by 25% when chained.
  • Steam Vent: an overload vents first, scalding nearby monsters, and lasts half as long.
  • Red Hot: a maneuver close to overloading makes your automaton's next hit a critical.
  • A puppet with a personality: your automaton talks in your chat log, in a voice set by its head.
  • Fresh Oil, Severed Strings and Puppeteer's Footwork: Repair powers up your automaton, its loss powers you up, and fighting beside it builds TP.
