Promise 2 of 6

Your job, with new tricks

The job you love, deeper than ever.

Every job keeps its classic role and gains mechanics that reward playing it well. Each has a quest line for a weapon nobody else can earn, and twelve can master their two-hour.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

All 18 jobs

Every job, reworked

The classic identity stays; the new mechanics deepen it. Your companions learn the new tricks too.

Mastered two-hours

Twelve side quests

Finish your job quest, then talk to its giver again. They name an old foe: use your two-hour in the fight and bring it to its knees, and your two-hour is mastered for good.

JobTwo-hourMastered
ThiefPerfect DodgeEvery attack against you gives 30 TP; afterwards your next weapon skill strikes as Sneak and Trick Attack.
PaladinInvincibleNearby allies take 15% less physical damage, and blocked blows are stored and released as holy damage.
