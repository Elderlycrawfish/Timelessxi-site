New Player Guide

From your first step to level 75

Everything new, in the order you'll meet it.

Timeless XI is the level 75 game you remember with more than forty new systems on top. You don't need to learn them all at once. Follow the four stages below, and look up a command whenever you need it.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Day one

Four things to do first

1

Install and log in

Follow First Steps to install the Timeless XI Launcher. It sets up the game, the add-ons and the item windows for you.

2

Pick any job

Every job has been reworked and all of them can solo with companions. Choose the one you like the look of.

3

Call your companions

Outside town, type !companion. You'll have a tank, a healer, a support and two damage dealers.

4

Know the rules, then ask anything

Review the server rules, then ask questions in the Timeless linkshell or the Discord.

Your road to 75

What to do at each stage

The classic climb, with the new systems introduced as you reach them.

Levels 1 to 10Stage 1

First steps

  • Call your companions right away. Step outside town and type !companion for a full party, or /cm to pick jobs in a window. They level with you, so you never wait for a group.
  • Open the Records of Eminence menu. No Fields of Valor book to find: choose a training page for your zone from the menu anywhere, and every page you finish pays gil, tabs and EXP.
  • Pop every Treasure Casket. Any monster that gives EXP can leave one. Blue caskets pay gil and crafting materials, brown ones hold gear near your level, and the rare rainbow casket is an upgrade chosen for your job.
  • Say hello in the Timeless linkshell. Every adventurer has its pearl from the first login. Questions get answered fast.
  • Start a craft and a gathering skill. They level far faster than retail and bank rested synths while you're away. !craft and !gather show where you stand.
Levels 10 to 30Stage 2

Out into the world

  • Watch for Champions. About one monster in 200 is a Champion with special traits and better rewards. !champions look tells you what you're facing; Elite Champions appear from level 20, so bring the whole party.
  • Take the daily bounties. !bounty lists three hunts a day and two a week. They pay Everglass, guild standing and Hunter's Marks.
  • Hunt notorious monsters on a schedule. Every NM respawns on a set timer, with no placeholders. Plan your route with the NM Tracker; Leaping Lizzy and the Valkurm Emperor are classic first targets.
  • Step into a Time Rift if one opens. Rifts tear open near players in the field. Inside, your party fights an echo of a famous NM at your level for Everglass and its drops.
  • Keep your favorite companions. !companion keep saves one; from then on the same person answers every call, and your bond with them grows into real bonuses.
  • Unlock your support job at 18. The classic quest is unchanged. Companions can cover any role while you level the new job.
Levels 30 to 50Stage 3

Jeuno and beyond

  • Settle into Jeuno. The Artisans Guild in Lower Jeuno takes commissions and posts requests, the Wandering Merchant visits with rare stock, and named adventurers there will join your party.
  • Use your Eternal Sand. Monsters in 18 zones can drop it. Use it to step into the past with your party for bonus EXP, and watch for Beastman Warlords.
  • Fill your Mog House. Furniture powers bonuses everywhere you go, and your moogle has two small chores a day. !hearth and !chores.
  • Start The Bestiary. Quillon in Upper Jeuno, any job from level 30: a side quest that rewards you for the beasts you fight.
  • Alliances pay full EXP. Three players with companions can field a full 18-person alliance, and nobody loses EXP for it.
Levels 50 to 75Stage 4

Mastery

  • Begin your job quest line. Most open at 60 (a few at 50) and end with a weapon only your job can earn. See every quest on the jobs page; !journal tracks your steps.
  • Master your two-hour. Finish your job quest, then talk to its giver again for a side quest that upgrades your two-hour for good.
  • Go to Chains of Promathia areas with Signet. From level 50, Signet adds 5% to 10% EXP there.
  • Take on the legends. Collect echo shards from Time Rifts to open Ancient Rifts against Fafnir, Behemoth and Adamantoise, and later their kings.
  • Keep your gear in your wardrobes. All eight Mog Wardrobes are open from day one: a full set for every job.

Tips

  • Bring companions everywhere. They make every fight safer, and every worthy kill together raises your bond.
  • Craft from day one. Rested synths pile up while you're away, so even a few synths a day go a long way.
  • Go home now and then. Chores, the garden and your Hearth reward you for checking in.
  • Don't fight Elite or Legendary Champions alone. Read the traits first with !champions look.
  • Play through the launcher. It keeps the game patched, so quest rewards and items show their Timeless names and stats.
  • Found a bug? Note the time, your zone and what you did, and open a ticket in the Discord. See the rules.
