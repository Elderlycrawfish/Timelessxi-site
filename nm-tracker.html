Notorious Monster Tracker

Know when they're up.

Every notorious monster on Timeless XI respawns on a set timer, and there are no placeholders. No lottery pops and no hours of farming the wrong monster: when the timer runs out, the NM is back.

Use this list to plan your routes and hunt in real time. Search by name, zone or drop to see what's up, the countdown to each spawn, the primary drop and its drop rate, kills since it last dropped, and who defeated it last.

Live status turns on at launch, December 2026. Until then you can browse every NM, its respawn timer and its primary drop. Up / awaiting status, countdowns, kills since the last drop and the last victor appear once the server is open.

Primary drop = the highest-level piece of gear the NM drops (or its rarest item when it drops no gear); drop rates are the base rates from the server's drop tables with no Treasure Hunter (TH) applied, so Treasure Hunter will raise them; each item's pop-out also shows its rate at TH2, using the server's Treasure Hunter table. Drops that come from a guaranteed group (for example "always one of these") aren't changed by Treasure Hunter. Item details and icons are the game's own item text; click an item to open it on FFXIAH, or an NM's name to open its BG Wiki page. Hovering over an NM's name shows its picture from BG Wiki; where BG Wiki has no picture of that NM, it shows its monster family instead. Respawn = the NM's timer after it's defeated; time-of-day and weather requirements still apply. HNMs spawn in windows shown once live data is on. Racing for a claim is fair; see the rules on holding monsters for NM etiquette.
