Enhancement 6 of 6

Quality of Life

Less waiting, more adventuring.

Room for every job's gear, a Mog House that works for you, notorious monsters without the camp, and the old friction sanded away, so your time goes into adventuring.

COMING SOONDecember 2026

Storage that travels with you

Room for every job

Every adventurer has all eight Mog Wardrobes from the first day: 80 slots each, 640 in all, with no unlock quests. They travel with you, and you can equip straight from them anywhere in Vana'diel.

  • A full set for every job. Keep each job's gear in its own wardrobe and change jobs without a trip home.
  • Porter Moogles in the cities store whole armor sets on storage slips, freeing even more space.
  • Generous stack sizes: hundreds of items that never stacked in the original era, from ores to beastmen's spoils, now stack to 12 (some to 99).
  • Your bags stay free for loot, crystals and crafting materials.

Notorious monsters without the camp

Set timers, no placeholders

219 notorious monsters respawn on a set timer instead of from placeholders. No hours of killing the wrong monster hoping for a pop: when the timer is up, the NM is back. Time-of-day and weather rules still apply.

A Mog House that works for you

The Hearth

The furniture you place powers bonuses everywhere you go. Each piece adds points to its element, and pieces crafters can make count double. At 12, 30 and 60 points an element reaches tiers 1, 2 and 3.

Furniture sets

Eight sets each add a perk when complete, like an extra Hunter's Mark per bounty or longer Champion lures.

Daily life at home

Moogle chores

Two small chores a day, from a few synths to a bounty or a harvest. Each pays Mog Tokens for rare furniture, seeds, flowerpots, chocobo treats and rested synths.

!chores · !moogle

Garden Plus

Gardening levels 1 to 100: more crops, a chance to get your seeds back, and plants that take longer to wilt. The right plant, crystal and pot can crossbreed a rare crop.

!garden pots · !garden crosses

Welcome back

One login summary of everything that happened while you were away: errands, commissions, your chocobo, your garden, the expedition, bounties and more.
