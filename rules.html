Server rules

Rules of Timeless XI

Play fair, play kind, and look out for each other. Vana'diel is a world we share.

By playing on Timeless XI you agree to these rules, and to the Discord rules if you use our Discord. You must be at least 16 years old to make an account. Staff may collect your email address, IP addresses, account and character names, and game activity and chat logs, only to stop cheating and keep the game fair, and delete them once they're no longer needed.

The rules

  1. One account per person, one game at a time
  2. No real money trading (RMT)
  3. No ban evasion
  4. No exploits
  5. Approved add-ons only
  6. Game file (DAT) changes
  7. No bots or automation
  8. No monster player kills (MPK)
  9. Listen to the GMs
  10. No harassment or hate speech
  11. No griefing
  12. Holding monsters
  13. Out-of-era items
  14. Leaked staff information
  15. Advertising
  16. Gardening and crafting

Questions? Ask in the Discord
1

One account per person, one game at a time

Timeless XI is a single-box server. Each person may have one account, and each account is tied to the IP addresses that use it. An account holds up to three characters.

Running more than one account from the same IP address is not allowed without an exception from staff. That includes household members sharing an IP, and "functional dual boxing": a second account whose only purpose is to help yours (for example, following you around to carry items or sell for you). Staff decide what counts as functional dual boxing.

Account sharing is not allowed, including two people taking turns on one account, or lending your account to someone who hides their IP with a VPN. Every account involved may be banned.

You don't need a second account: your AI companions fill your party.

2

No real money trading (RMT)

Buying, selling or trading gil, items, characters or services on Timeless XI for real money, or for currency or items from any other game or platform, is forbidden.

Don't accept gil or items from players you don't know. If the sender turns out to be an RMT seller, your account may be actioned too. Use Return in the delivery box instead.

3

No ban evasion

A banned player may not come back on a new account or character. New accounts found to belong to a banned player are banned as well.

Knowingly helping a banned player rebuild, for example with large gifts of gil or items, may lead to action against your account. Staff decide what counts as large.

4

No exploits

Exploiting bugs or game mechanics is forbidden. That includes glitched or unbalanced content, abusing monster pathing, and unintended interactions between monster AI and jobs, abilities or companions.

Found something broken? Report it with a ticket in the Discord. If you're not sure whether something is a bug, ask before you use it. Penalties for exploiting are at staff discretion.

5

Approved add-ons only

Only approved add-ons and third-party tools may be used. The approved list is what the Timeless XI Launcher installs, plus anything staff list in the Discord. If a tool isn't on the list, it isn't allowed.

Want a tool reviewed? Ask in the Discord. Add-ons you write yourself must be made public before staff will consider them. Using unapproved tools can lead to a suspension or a permanent ban.

6

Game file (DAT) changes

Cosmetic and convenience changes such as higher-resolution textures, labeled maps and custom music are fine.

You must keep the Timeless XI game patches installed. The Timeless XI Launcher installs and updates them for you, so always play through the launcher.

Changes that give you an unfair edge, such as removing monsters or obstacles to claim faster, are not allowed.

7

No bots or automation

Every action your character takes must come from you. Claim bots, farming bots, skill-up bots, heal bots, quest bots and any other script or program that plays for you are forbidden.

This includes gear-swap rules that react to the game for you, such as automatically changing gear in response to a monster's TP move. Swapping gear when you press a key or use an action is fine.

Your AI companions are part of the game and are always allowed. Their behavior is run by the server, not by tools on your computer.

8

No monster player kills (MPK)

Don't use monsters or their abilities to harm, harass or kill other players on purpose, including deliberately attacking a player who has been charmed by a monster. Staff decide what counts as MPK.

9

Listen to the GMs

If a Game Master gives you an instruction, follow it. GMs checking for automation may move you or stop your action without a message first, and you're expected to respond by moving or replying.

If you think a GM got it wrong, open a ticket in the Discord to discuss it with staff. Don't argue it out in game.

10

No harassment or hate speech

Harassment, witch hunts, sexism, racism, threats of violence and hate speech are not tolerated, in any chat channel, search comment or bazaar comment. Hate speech means language attacking anyone for their race, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, disability, age, health or any similar trait.

Swearing aimed at another player in a harassing way counts too. Leave real-world politics out of the game.

11

No griefing

Don't set out to spoil someone else's game for your own amusement or gain. Joking around is fine, but stop when you're asked to, and never act with clear malice. Staff judge each case on its own facts.

Holding a Dynamis zone long after you've finished, or spamming trades or spells on players to block their entry, counts as griefing.

12

Holding monsters

Don't hold a monster for more than five minutes unless you mean to kill it. Holding one while you wait for help is fine. Holding monsters to delay a spawn timer or to get in other players' way is not.

Notorious monsters on Timeless XI respawn on set timers. Racing for the claim is fair; blocking, training monsters onto other players, or tagging a monster you can't fight to keep it from others is not.

13

Out-of-era items

Items from later expansions that slipped into the game by mistake may not be used or sold. Report any you find with a ticket in the Discord. If you're not sure whether an item belongs in this era, ask.

14

Leaked staff information

If you come across leaked staff information, report it in the Discord and don't share it.

15

Advertising

Don't spam public chat or send unsolicited tells advertising things outside the game, such as other servers, streams or links, without staff permission. Recruiting for your linkshell in game is fine.

16

Gardening and crafting

Garden and craft only on the up to three characters of your own account. Using extra accounts or characters to grow or craft for you is not allowed.

17

Names

Every name you can choose, for your character, linkshell, bazaar or anything else, must be appropriate. Names with political content, sexual content or innuendo, or anything covered by the hate speech rule will have to be changed. Staff make the call.

18

GM tickets

Open GM tickets in the Timeless XI Discord. Only the players affected by an issue should file it; tickets filed on someone else's behalf may be closed.

19

Enforcement

Staff may suspend or close any account, or remove access to the server, at their discretion, including for breaking these rules. These rules may change at any time, and not knowing a rule is never an excuse.

Last updated November 10, 2026. Staff may update these rules at any time; check back before launch in December 2026.
