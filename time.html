Echoes of Time

Echoes of Time

Time is coming apart. Step through.

Rifts tear open in the field. Sand that should not exist falls from monsters. And in Dynamis, a war that ended long ago is still being fought, every night, in its dreams.

JOIN THE BETAOpens December 2026

Time Rifts

A rift opens beside you

Inside, an old legend is waiting. It knows your level.

Step through with your party and face an echo of a famous notorious monster, rebuilt at your level and never quite the same twice. Leaping Lizzy. The Valkurm Emperor. Simurgh. Beat it before time runs out.

  • Echo shards from the echoes you defeat.
  • Ancient Rifts: gather enough shards and Fafnir, Behemoth and Adamantoise answer.
  • And then their kings.

Eternal Sand

A monster falls, and something glitters

Step into the past, or let it scatter. You have sixty seconds.

Grains of Eternal Sand carry your party twenty years back, to the war that shaped Vana'diel. The monsters rise to meet you, experience flows faster, and sometimes a Beastman Warlord takes the field.

  • Warlords of the Orcs, Quadav, Yagudo and the Shadow Lord's army.
  • The Last Grains: the final minutes of every visit burn brightest.
  • The Sands Converge: when the server uses enough sand in a day, the past opens wide.

Dynamis

Dreams of a war long past

Open every night, to everyone. Bring one party, or five.

Buy a Prismatic Hourglass from the hourglass goblins and step into all ten dreams, from the four cities to Tavnazia. Every entry starts your own two hours fresh. No reservations, no waiting a day between runs.

  • A relic grind that respects your time: currency drops outright, and every seventh notorious monster brings a 100-piece.
  • Upgrades in a minute, not days.
  • Ancient Tokens for your spare relic armour.
  • New nightmares stalk Dynamis - Tavnazia.
